4. Cookies and analytics

We do not use our own cookies, analytics tools, advertising trackers, embedded social media content or externally loaded fonts on this website. The website contains no contact form.

This preference is used solely for presentation, contains no personal data and is not transmitted to us. Without a manual selection, the website automatically follows your device's system setting.
